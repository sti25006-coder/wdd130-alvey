Explore the Course

Our campus course winds through the beautiful scenery of BYU-Idaho. Use the guide below to plan your round.

Hole 1

The Open Start

Par 3 | 240ft

A straightforward shot across the lawn. This is the perfect warm-up hole, but keep it low to stay under the wind.

Hole 2

The Pine Gap

Par 3 | 195ft

A technical shot through two large pine trees. Accuracy is more important than power on this short par 3.

Hole 3

Hillside Toss

Par 3 | 310ft

Throwing slightly uphill toward the stadium. Be careful of "rollaways" if your disc lands on its edge.

Hole 4

The Sidewalk Squeeze

Par 3 | 215ft

A narrow fairway with walking paths on both sides. Stay center to avoid going Out of Bounds!

Hole 5

The Long Bomber

Par 3 | 380ft

The longest hole on the campus course. Put everything you've got into this drive toward the open field.

Hole 6

Basket in the Trees

Par 3 | 180ft

Don't let the short distance fool you; the basket is tucked behind heavy branches that eat discs for breakfast.

Hole 7

The Corner Hook

Par 3 | 225ft

A "dogleg" right. You'll need a disc that fades hard at the end to get parked near the pin.

Hole 8

Stadium View

Par 3 | 260ft

Throwing back toward the stadium. Watch out for wind gusts that come off the large buildings nearby.

Hole 9

Final Flight

Par 3 | 210ft

The finishing hole. A clear shot that brings you right back to where you started for another round.
